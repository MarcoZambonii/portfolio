IDEA 02 · THE STUDIO

From materials
to a study path.

Pick a course — your notes plus the WeBeep files — and the Studio turns it into a study: a summary, flashcards, exercises with correction. Every quote is checked literally against the source before being marked VERIFIED; math is rendered in LaTeX.

Two calls per MODULE, not per exercise: the re-solve sends the whole set in one call, so a set of eight exercises costs 2 calls, not 16. Two independent mechanisms: the re-solve discards what doesn't hold, the quote check labels what can't be found — and it uses no AI at all. Safety valve: if the re-solve rejects EVERY exercise, the app assumes the verification went wrong and keeps the originals unmarked. Tolerances are deliberate: case and spacing ignored, a second attempt ignoring symbols (the same formula can be written in different ways), and quotes under 12 characters are not considered at all. Regeneration is never automatic — the student triggers it with a comment on what's wrong.

IDEA 03 · THE CHAIN

Same five models.
Two different chains.

Quota is per model, so chaining five means 1,060 calls a day instead of 500. The order follows the job: Lite models first where volume matters (reading, summaries), all three full Flash first where reasoning matters (exercises) — and if a Lite ends up generating one, the card says so.

A failed attempt never consumes daily quota — only a successful answer does, so breaking a link burns nothing and retrying is free. The chain truly stops in three cases only: no network, wrong or missing API key, user cancel. If all five daily quotas are gone: "All models exhausted today's quota. Resets at Pacific midnight — 9:00 in Italy."

IDEA 04 · PARALLELISM

Together,
not in sequence.

Modules don't depend on each other, so total time isn't the sum — it's the slowest module. And the slowest is always Exercises: the only one on a capable model, spending two calls (generation plus anti-hallucination verification) with reasoning switched on. The other three are extractive tasks on a fast model and finish in a couple of seconds, freeing slots before they're needed. The cap of three isn't about rate limits: past three slots there is nothing to gain, because the three fast modules finish within seconds and the slow one is left alone anyway.

IDEA 05 · THE CONSTRAINT

Costs scale on the student.
Never on the developer.

The app must stay free with many users — so no centralized anything. No secrets in the code, no backend to maintain, nothing that bills me when you study more.

100% FREE · NEARLY ZERO COMPROMISES
CLIENT-SIDE AI · BYOK Everyone uses their own key: Gemini free tier by default, Claude optional, Apple on-device as zero-config fallback.
CLIENT-SIDE WEBEEP SSO + MFA login in the browser; the app only holds a Keychain token. Credentials never touch third-party servers.
LOCAL-FIRST Data lives in SwiftData on the device. No mandatory backend, no server bill, no account.

On the all-free setup — Gemini free tier + Apple on-device + Wolfram free AppID — everything works: ink, WeBeep, Studio, magic pen. The only trade-off is the daily call quota, and the chain exists precisely to make it a non-issue.

IDEA 06 · THE INK

240 Hz.
Ink built from scratch.

Coalesced touches and prediction from the Apple Pencil, vector rendering, pressure-to-width laws measured empirically. PencilKit survives only as a storage format. Hundred-page dispense stay fluid thanks to tiled rendering with a residency window.

MULTIPLY HIGHLIGHTER OBJECT ERASER LASSO + MOVE LATEX OBJECTS WOLFRAM · DESMOS

SwiftUI · iPadOS 18+ · Apple Pencil. Open the Xcode project and run — no dependencies to resolve. A personal project, in active development; not affiliated with Politecnico di Milano or Moodle/WeBeep.
