Recovering the Risk-Neutral Density Across Maturities

A probabilistic and computational application of the Breeden–Litzenberger result: implied volatility extraction, SVI calibration, an arbitrage-free call price surface, and finite differences to reconstruct the density — with full Python pipeline and validation against the true density.

Theory-Driven vs. Data-Driven Models in Demand Forecasting

B.Sc. research project carried out at Irbema S.r.l. on behalf of PoliMi: Holt, Croston TSB and ARIMA against Random Forest and XGBoost on a ~300-SKU portfolio, with leakage-free walk-forward validation. Outcome: no universal winner — a per-article decision scheme routing each demand profile to its best model.

WORK IN PROGRESS

Odds as Implied Probabilities: Efficiency in Betting Markets

De-vigging, Poisson inversion of the Over/Under ladder into an implied goal intensity λ, then validation walk-forward, against closing lines, and on live Betfair in-play data. Finding so far: the market is efficient within transaction costs — the one surviving angle is structural (time decay, liquidity), not informational.
